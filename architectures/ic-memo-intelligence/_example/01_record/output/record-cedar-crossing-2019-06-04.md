# Decision Record: Cedar Crossing Apartments
Decision: Approved-with-conditions   Decided: 2019-06-04
Asset type: Multifamily   Deal-size band: $25M–$75M   Strategy: Value-add
Market/submarket: Phoenix (Tempe)

## Recommended vs. Decided
Memo recommended acquisition at $41.5M, 68% LTV, a 5-year value-add hold underwritten to 16.0% net
IRR / 1.9x, on a 12-month renovation-and-lease-up plan. Committee **approved, but stepped the
leverage down to 65% LTV and attached three conditions.** The deal cleared; the structure did not
clear as proposed.

## Conditions Imposed
- Leverage capped at 65% LTV (from 68%) | Leverage cap / max LTV | deal team, before close
- DSCR stress test at +200 bps to underwritten rate, must hold ≥1.20x | Debt-service / DSCR stress test | underwriting, before close
- $1.5M capex/lease-up reserve funded at close | Reserve or contingency requirement | deal team, at close

## Concerns Raised
- Lease-up / absorption risk | Lease-up / absorption risk | "Twelve months to stabilize a full-reno
  on this unit count is the optimistic end; what happens to the return if it's eighteen?" (Okafor)
- Leverage / financing risk | Leverage / financing risk | committee "not comfortable at 68% on a
  value-add business plan in this rate environment."

## Dissent
None formal. One stated reservation (Okafor) that the lease-up assumption was aggressive; satisfied
by the reserve and DSCR conditions rather than carried to a no-vote.

## Vote
Approved; one reservation noted in the minutes (Okafor). Not a split vote.

## Gaps
The minutes note the committee "discussed the sponsor's prior Tempe execution" but do not record
what was concluded — analysis should not infer the sponsor track record was decisive from this
line alone.
