# Hold / Sell / Refi Workspace

## What This Is
A decision workspace for evaluating whether an owned asset should be held, sold, refinanced, recapitalized, or revisited later. It frames alternatives and decision conditions from platform-verified data and model outputs.

This is not a valuation engine or disposition platform. The model and brokers own valuation and proceeds math. Fund administration owns capital-return mechanics. This workspace owns the investment judgment and IC-ready framing.
