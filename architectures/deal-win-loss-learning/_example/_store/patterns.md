# Ridgeline Bid Patterns

Rolled-up intelligence across the bid records. This is the file the next bid — and deal-pipeline
sourcing / bid strategy — reads first. A pattern is "stated" at 3+ supporting records; below that
it is "emerging." Records are append-only; patterns are revised in place with dated notes.

Records in store: 3 (Cypress Logistics Park — won; Hawthorne Town Center — lost; Larkspur
Apartments — lost).

---

## STATED PATTERNS (3+ records)

### P1 — We win on certainty of close, not top price; losses trace to certainty/execution doubts even when the broker cites price
**Segments:** all asset types so far; clean-execution sellers (developer, fund in wind-down,
long-hold family).
**Evidence:** 3 records.
- Cypress (won): won ~$2.5M *below* the top bid on a no-contingency, 30-day, hard-deposit package.
- Hawthorne (lost): lost to a buyer who waived the financing contingency; broker cited price.
- Larkspur (lost): lost to a waived-contingency / larger-deposit buyer; broker cited price, but the
  term gap exceeded the 3% price gap.
**Confidence:** High. Consistent across a win and two losses, and the two "broker said price"
losses both resolved to certainty on inspection. Caution: certainty packaging is entangled with
process type (see P2).
**Last updated:** 2026-05-12 (Larkspur added; promoted from emerging to stated).
**Action:** Build bids around the certainty package — pre-clear or lock debt before best-and-final
so we can waive the financing contingency, and lead with a larger hard deposit. Treat a financing
contingency carried into best-and-final as a red flag on clean-execution sellers.

## EMERGING PATTERNS (<3 records)

### P2 — Broadly-marketed processes where we carry a financing contingency go against us; off-market / bilateral with a clean package go our way
**Segments:** all asset types.
**Evidence:** 2 records, plus the Cypress contrast.
- Off-market + clean package: Cypress (won).
- Marketed/limited + contingency: Hawthorne (limited, lost), Larkspur (broadly marketed, lost).
**Confidence:** Medium. Directionally clear but **entangled with P1** — in every loss we both
faced a competitive process *and* carried a contingency, so process type and certainty packaging
are confounded. Needs a record where we carry a clean package into a broadly-marketed process to
isolate the effect.
**Last updated:** 2026-05-12.
**Watch:** the next broadly-marketed deal where we bid a clean, no-contingency package — does it
win? That record separates P2 from P1.

### P3 — Pinnacle Realty Advisors' debrief skews to "price" and masks the real certainty driver
**Segments:** broker-reliability (Pinnacle).
**Evidence:** 2 records (Hawthorne, Larkspur). Hypothesis.
**Confidence:** Low-medium — 2 records, both losses, both where the assessed driver was certainty
but Pinnacle stated price.
**Last updated:** 2026-05-12.
**Watch:** the next Pinnacle process — does their stated reason again diverge from the assessed
driver? If a third record confirms, weight Pinnacle's price feedback as unreliable and corroborate
against winning-bid structure every time. (Internal to the acquisitions team — do not circulate a
named-broker reliability read outside it.)

## CONTRADICTIONS / OPEN QUESTIONS
- None outright contradict yet. The open question is the **P1/P2 entanglement**: are we losing
  because of the certainty *package* (P1) or the competitive *process type* (P2)? They co-occur in
  every record so far. The deciding evidence is a clean-package bid in a broadly-marketed process —
  prioritize capturing that record when it resolves.
