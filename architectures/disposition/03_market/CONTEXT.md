# Stage 03: Market

## Purpose
On an approved sell, prepare the asset for market and launch the process: set the disposition strategy, select the broker, build the disposition package and marketing narrative, and take it to market. This stage runs only when the decision stage approved a sell.

## Inputs
- **02_decision/output/**: The sell decision and the timing thesis that sets the strategy.
- **_config/disposition-standards.md**: Broker-selection criteria, required package contents, approval gates.
- **_config/asset-profile.md**: The asset detail the package draws on.
- **_references/**: Prior dispositions and broker relationships and track records.

## Process
1. Set the disposition strategy from the timing thesis: broad marketed process vs. targeted/off-market, the buyer pool to target, the pricing strategy and any guidance.
2. Select the broker against the standards: relevant track record in the asset type and submarket, the proposed process, fee. Document the rationale.
3. Build the disposition package — the GP's side of the marketing materials: the asset story, the business-plan achievement, the value drivers and the upside left for a buyer. The figures come from the model and the actuals; the narrative is the work here.
4. Prepare the buyer-facing narrative consistent with the package, and the data the broker will need.
5. Confirm the package meets the standards and clears the required internal approval before launch.
6. Launch and track the process: interest, tours, the offer timeline.
7. Record the strategy, broker selection, package, and launch in output.

## Output
Write to: 03_market/output/

```
# Disposition Strategy & Launch: [Asset]
Decision reference: 02_decision/output/

## Strategy
[Process type, target buyer pool, pricing strategy and guidance.]

## Broker Selection
[Selected broker and the rationale against the standards. Fee and process.]

## Disposition Package
[The asset story, business-plan achievement, value drivers, remaining upside.
 Figures sourced to the model and actuals; narrative is the deliverable.]

## Launch & Process Tracking
[Launch date, interest, tours, the offer deadline and timeline.]
```

## Done Looks Like
A launched process: a documented strategy, a selected broker with rationale, a package that meets the standards and cleared approval, and a tracked timeline to offers.

## Common Failure Modes
- **A package number that does not tie to the model or actuals.** Marketing latitude is in the narrative, never in the figures. Every number traces to source.
- **Skipping the broker-selection rationale.** "We always use them" is not a selection. Document the fit against the standards; it is a fiduciary decision.
- **Launching before approval.** The standards define the gate before market. Clear it; a process pulled back after launch is costly and visible.

## Layer Annotation
L2 stage contract. The sell decision is L4. Disposition standards and asset profile from _config/ are L3. Prior dispositions and broker records from _references/ are L3.
