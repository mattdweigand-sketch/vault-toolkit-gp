## How We Sound
[3-5 patterns in the firm's writing — not adjectives ("transparent," "confident"), actual patterns.
"States the headline number in the first sentence." "Names the asset and the issue directly rather
than abstracting it into 'headwinds.'" "Explains a problem before the reader has to ask, paired with

## How We Handle Bad News
[The firm's approach to delivering a miss, a markdown, or uncertainty. Lead with it or contextualize

## What Our Voice Is NOT
[The boundaries that catch drift, everywhere the firm writes. "Not promotional (no 'incredible,'

eliminate the most common failure directions.]
