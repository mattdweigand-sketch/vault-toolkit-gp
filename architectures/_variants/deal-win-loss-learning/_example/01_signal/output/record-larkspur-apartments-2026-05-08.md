# Bid Record: Larkspur Apartments
Outcome: Lost   Resolved: 2026-05-08
Asset type: Multifamily   Deal-size band: $25M–$75M
Process type: Broadly marketed   Seller type: Private / family
Broker/intermediary: Pinnacle Realty Advisors   Market/submarket: Phoenix (Tempe)

## How It Reached Us
Broadly marketed offering. Pinnacle sent the OM to the institutional buyer list on 2026-03-09;
we were one of ~40 recipients. No prior relationship with this seller (a local family that built
and held the asset for 18 years).

## Our Bid
Thesis: value-add multifamily — interior renovations on classic units, mark-to-market on ~$180/mo
loss-to-lease, amenity upgrade. Best-and-final bid: $63.0M. Terms: 45-day close, 15-day financing
contingency, deposit 2% at PSA going hard after the contingency period. Debt: agency, quote in
hand but not locked.

## Clearing Price & Our Gap
Broker-reported clearing: ~$65.0M (~3% / ~$2.0M above us). Winning terms (per Pinnacle): financing
contingency waived, 30-day close, larger non-refundable deposit (~5% hard at signing). Source:
Pinnacle debrief call, 2026-05-08 — not public record; deposit figure is broker color, not
confirmed.

## Broker's Stated Reason
Pinnacle (verbatim, close): "You guys were right there — about three percent light on price.
Strong group, the seller liked you, it just came down to the number."

## Process Path
First round: advanced (top ~8). Best-and-final: top 3. Lost at best-and-final; no last-look
offered. We were invited to "sharpen" once before BAFO and moved from $61.5M to $63.0M.

## Gaps
- The winning bid's exact deposit structure is broker color, not confirmed in writing.
- We did not get a last look, so we don't know whether matching the price (or improving terms)
  would have reopened it.
- We never spoke with the seller directly; all read on seller priorities is through Pinnacle.
