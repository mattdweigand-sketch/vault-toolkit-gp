# Code of Conduct

This project uses a simple standard: be clear, constructive, and respectful.

## Expected Behavior

- Discuss ideas on their merits.
- Give specific, actionable feedback.
- Assume good intent, but correct problems directly.
- Respect privacy and do not post confidential data.
- Keep examples safe to publish.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Posting secrets, customer data, or confidential business material.
- Repeatedly derailing issues or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; reject pull requests; or block contributors when needed to keep the project useful and safe.
