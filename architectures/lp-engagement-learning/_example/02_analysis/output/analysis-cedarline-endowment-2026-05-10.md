## Why (proposed)
The engagement was lost at the terms-and-access stage, not the strategy stage. [Inference,
medium-high confidence] The value-add thesis landed — they ran full diligence. What killed it
was that the GP-commitment and fee objections were handled in writing and the firm never reached
the actual decision-makers, so conviction never consolidated. [Inference, medium confidence] The
"allocation full" stated reason is partly a graceful exit. The cold origin made the whole thing
fragile: no relationship capital to spend when the terms questions arrived.

## Against the Store
Extends an emerging pattern: cold-sourced institutions stalling at the terms/access stage, in
contrast to the two committed records (Glenwood re-up, Harborstone referral), both warm and both
reaching the decision-maker. Also extends the "terms questions from endowments/foundations are a
gating signal" thread. Does not contradict any existing pattern.

## What We'd Do Differently
- On the first terms objection from a cold institutional LP, escalate to a partner and request
  time with the decision-maker — do not answer in writing and wait.
- Treat "GP commitment + fee step-down" questions as a conviction signal that needs a live
  conversation, not a document.
