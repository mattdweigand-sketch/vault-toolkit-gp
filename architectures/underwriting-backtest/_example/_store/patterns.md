"stated" at 3+ supporting records; below that it is "emerging." Records are append-only; patterns

Records in store: 3 (Cedar Crossing — MF, outperformed; Junction 18 — industrial, outperformed;

## STATED PATTERNS (3+ records)

### P1 — Our realized outperformance has leaned on exit-cap compression we did not underwrite and did not create; it is market beta, not repeatable skill
**Segments:** all asset types and markets so far (MF and industrial; Phoenix, Tucson, Inland Empire);
2018–2020 vintages exiting 2023–2024.
**Direction / magnitude:** exit caps compressed 45–70 bps below underwriting in every record.
**Evidence:** 3 records.
- Cedar Crossing: −65 bps; the compression supplied the entire IRR beat (luck-dominant).
- Junction 18: −70 bps; cap plus a sector rent surge (mixed).
- Maple Grove: −45 bps; the compression was the only reason a poorly-executed deal still beat
  (luck-dominant).
**Confidence:** High. Consistent across a full deployment-and-exit cycle. The compression is a
rate-cycle artifact, not a Ridgeline edge.
**Last updated:** 2024-09-16 (Cedar Crossing added; promoted from emerging to stated).
**Action / calibration:** Underwrite exit caps **flat-to-+25 bps over going-in**; do not assume
compression. Treat any deal whose underwriting *requires* cap compression to clear the hurdle as a
red flag. (Flows to deal-screening economics-assumptions and deal-pipeline underwriting.)

Junction 18 — so this is MF-specific.)
**Direction / magnitude:** actual stabilization runs 6–10 months longer than the standard 12-month
assumption.
**Evidence:** 2 records (both MF).

to promote.
**Last updated:** 2024-09-16.
**Watch / calibration:** Move the MF value-add full-reno stabilization assumption toward ~18 months
(from 12). Confirm on the next MF realization before hard-coding fund-wide.

## SKILL-VS-LUCK LEDGER (internal to deal team + IC — do not circulate)
Across three realized "outperformances": **2 luck-dominant** (Cedar Crossing, Maple Grove),
**1 mixed** (Junction 18), **0 skill-dominant**. The honest read: our recent track record is more
market beta than execution alpha. The one genuine, repeatable judgment call in the store is the
Inland Empire industrial market-selection thesis (Junction 18). This ledger is the reason to keep
underwriting conservatively rather than extrapolating the headline IRRs into the next fund's
assumptions.
