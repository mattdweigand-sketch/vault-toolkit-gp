# Source Hierarchy

## Book of record
For Maple Commons, the fund-administration platform is the book of record for the asset's current
basis, the outstanding debt balance, and the as-of NAV (S01). The financial model is the source
for forward projections and the hold/sell return math — but the model is a working tool, not the
book of record, and the two versions in the pile disagree (see the conflict log). No figure in
the deliverable is adjudicated against fund admin; where the model and fund admin would conflict
on a current figure, fund admin wins.

## Authority ladder
- **Authoritative** — `S01` fund admin export (current basis, debt, NAV, as-of 2026-03-31).
- **Supporting** — `S03` financial model FINAL (forward projections, hold/sell math); `S05`
  Q1 2026 asset management report (occupancy, NOI, business-plan status). Supporting because they
  inform the case but are not the system of record for the figures they carry.
- **Background** — `S04` CBRE broker opinion of value (a sale-price view, but a broker's, with the
  incentives that implies); `S06` internal email thread (informal, undated assumptions).
- **Superseded** — `S02` financial model v3 (replaced by S03 FINAL; kept for history, never cited
  as current); `S07` 2024 appraisal (two years stale; pre-dates the current business plan).

## Sensitive sources
None in this set. Were the pile to include LP-identifying capital-account detail or a
counterparty's confidential terms, those would be noted here and their contents never copied or
quoted into the inventory, the summaries, or the draft.

## Where the sources came from
A shared-drive folder the deal lead assembled by hand, plus two files forwarded by email. There
is no document register and no version control — which is exactly why the duplicate and version
analysis is the highest-value part of the inventory here. The two model files have similar names
(`...-model-v3` and `...-model-FINAL`) and no internal version stamp; the pass had to read both to
establish which is current and where they diverge. See Constraint 10.
