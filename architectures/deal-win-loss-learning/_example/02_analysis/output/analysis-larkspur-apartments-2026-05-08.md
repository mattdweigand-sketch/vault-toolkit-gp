# Win/Loss Analysis: Larkspur Apartments
Record reference: 01_signal/output/record-larkspur-apartments-2026-05-08.md   Outcome: Lost

## Canonical Questions
1. **How it reached us:** Broadly marketed; OM to ~40 buyers via Pinnacle. No prior seller
   relationship.
2. **Our thesis / bid:** Value-add multifamily (reno + loss-to-lease mark-to-market). Bid $63.0M,
   45-day close, 15-day financing contingency, agency debt quoted not locked.
3. **Clearing / gap:** ~$65.0M (~3% / ~$2.0M above us). In *terms*, our gap was larger than in
   price: winner waived the financing contingency, closed in 30 days, posted a ~5% hard deposit
   vs. our 2%.
4. **Won/lost on:** Assessed certainty/speed of close. Broker says price.
5. **Stated vs. assessed:** Stated — "3% light on price." Assessed — certainty of close was
   decisive. A family seller selling a long-held asset prizes a clean, fast, fully-committed
   close; the winner's waived contingency + 30-day + larger hard deposit is a materially more
   certain package than ours, and that gap is bigger than the 3% on price. I believe the assessed
   reason: the term structure is too lopsided for price alone to explain it. [medium-high]
6. **Full information?** Mostly. We bid knowing it was competitive and price-led, but we did *not*
   know how aggressively the field would compete on certainty terms (deposit size, waived
   contingency). Partial blind on the terms bar.
7. **Where we lost ground:** Best-and-final. We advanced cleanly through the first round and were
   top-3; the gap opened at BAFO, where we improved price ($61.5M→$63.0M) but did not materially
   strengthen terms.
8. **Diligence/closing reputation:** Neutral-to-helpful — Pinnacle noted "the seller liked you."
   It did not overcome the weaker certainty package.
9. **What would have changed it:** Waiving or shortening the financing contingency and posting a
   larger hard deposit, likely more than another 1–2% on price. Walk-away: above ~$66M the
   business plan return falls below our hurdle, so this stops being our deal around there.
10. **(N/A — lost.)**
11. **Do differently:** At BAFO on a clean-execution seller, compete on the certainty package, not
    just price — pre-clear or waive financing and lead with deposit, instead of nudging the number.

## Stated vs. Assessed Reason
Pinnacle stated we were "about 3% light on price." Assessed real reason: **certainty of close.**
The winning bid's term gap (waived financing out, 30-day close, ~5% hard deposit) is wider than its
3% price premium, and a long-hold family seller optimizing for a clean exit weights those terms
heavily. I do not collapse these: price was a *real* factor, but secondary. This is the second time
Pinnacle's "light on price" framing has diverged from the assessed driver (see Hawthorne).

## Why (proposed)
Lost at the certainty/terms bar in a broadly-marketed process. [Inference, medium-high] Our price
was competitive; our *terms* were not — the financing contingency and 45-day close read as less
certain than a buyer who waived the out and posted a bigger deposit. [Inference, medium] The
broadly-marketed format meant the seller could hold out for both a strong price *and* clean terms,
so a contingency we might carry in a limited process became disqualifying here. Causal: the terms
gap. Incidental: the 3% price gap (real, but not what decided it).

## Against the Store
**Strengthens P1** ("we win on certainty, lose on certainty doubts even when the broker cites
price"): Larkspur is a third record on this thread — Cypress won on certainty, Hawthorne lost on a
financing contingency, Larkspur lost the same way with the broker again citing price. This should
**promote P1 to a stated pattern (3 records).** Also strengthens the broker-reliability thread on
Pinnacle (price-skewed feedback). Does not contradict any pattern. Note the **P1/P2 entanglement**:
Larkspur was broadly marketed *and* we carried a contingency — process type and certainty packaging
are still confounded.

## What We'd Do Differently
- On clean-execution sellers, lead the BAFO with the certainty package (waive/pre-clear financing,
  larger hard deposit), not a price nudge.
- Lock or pre-clear debt *before* best-and-final on broadly-marketed deals so we can waive the
  financing contingency credibly.
