# Workflow: Market Thesis To Investment Box

## Overview
Three-stage workflow: Research -> Synthesis -> Box Update.

## Stage Map

| Stage | Purpose | Inputs | Output Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01_research | Gather and vet market inputs | Data providers, broker research, news, prior thesis | 01_research/output/ |
| 02_synthesis | Form the market view | Research pack, research standards | 02_synthesis/output/ |
| 03_box_update | Convert view into sourcing/screening changes | Thesis, current investment box | 03_box_update/output/ |

## AI vs. Platform

| Step | Layer | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Market data and transactions | Platform / data source | CoStar, PitchBook, market providers, broker data |
| Data cuts and calculations | Deterministic | Data provider exports / analyst model |
| Synthesis and implication framing | AI | Investment team |
| Investment-box change | Human | Investment lead / IC |
