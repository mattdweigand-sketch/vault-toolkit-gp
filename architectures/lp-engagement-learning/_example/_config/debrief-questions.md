## The Questions
Answer all of these, in order, for every resolved engagement — commit or pass.

1. How did this LP first enter the Fund III pipeline, and through whom?
2. What was the LP's stated mandate and target allocation for a fund like this?
3. Which of our materials or moments most moved the engagement forward?
4. What objections or concerns did the LP raise, and in what order?
5. What was the real decision driver, as distinct from the stated reason?
6. Who on the LP side actually held the decision, and did we reach them?
7. Where did the engagement stall or lose momentum, if it did?
8. What did our terms (fee, GP commitment, structure) contribute to the outcome?
9. What did timing — theirs and ours — contribute?
10. If passed: what, if anything, would have changed the outcome?
11. If committed: what sealed it, and what nearly lost it?
12. What would we do differently with a similar LP next time?

## Answering Rules
- Answer every question. Use "unknown" where the engagement record does not support an
  answer — never fabricate to fill the slot.
- Keep answers tight and factual; the "why" and the lessons have their own sections in the
  analysis output.
- Always distinguish the stated reason from the assessed reason (questions 5 and 8 especially).
