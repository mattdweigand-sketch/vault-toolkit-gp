# S05 — Maple Commons Asset Management Report, Q1 2026 (2026-03-31)

- **Type:** Asset-management quarterly report. **Authority:** Supporting.
- **Trustworthy for:** the operating picture — occupancy, in-place NOI trend, and business-plan
  status as of quarter-end.

## What it contains
- **Occupancy:** 93% as of 2026-03-31, up from 89% a year prior. The renovation-and-relet plan is
  largely complete; the remaining units are the slower interior-unit turns.
- **Rents:** renovated units achieving the underwritten premium (roughly +12% over pre-reno);
  trailing NOI up year over year.
- **Business plan:** ahead on occupancy, on plan on rents. The value-add thesis has substantially
  played out — which is itself part of the sell case (most of the lift has been captured).
- **Watch items:** two HVAC systems flagged for replacement in the next 18 months (relevant to the
  hold-case capital reserve, MISSING-03).

## Use in the deliverable
Cite S05 for the operating story: why the asset is in good shape, why the value-add plan is mostly
done, and what the hold case would still need to spend. It supports both cases — the sell case
("the lift is captured, harvest it") and the hold case ("a stabilized asset with durable income").
It is the operating evidence; the return math is S03's.
