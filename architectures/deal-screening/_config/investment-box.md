## Asset Types
[What the firm buys and explicitly does not. Be specific: multifamily,
industrial, grocery-anchored retail; not office; etc.]

## Geography
[Target markets and submarkets. Markets the firm will not enter. Note any
"opportunistic exception" rule if one exists, and who can invoke it.]

## Deal Size
[Equity check range, total capitalization range. The minimum below which a
deal is not worth the overhead, and the maximum above which it is out of scope.]

## Strategy / Profile
[Core, core-plus, value-add, opportunistic — which the firm runs. The business
plan archetypes it executes (e.g., lease-up, renovation, repositioning).]

## Return Profile
[Target return characteristics at the strategy level — the hurdle a deal must
plausibly clear to be worth pursuing. This is the bar, not an underwrite.]

## Structural Preferences
[Ownership structures, JV vs. direct, debt assumptions, hold-period
expectations, and any structures the firm will not do.]
