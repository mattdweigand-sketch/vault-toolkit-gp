# SKILL: Market Thesis To Investment Box Workspace Builder

## Description
Builds a workspace that turns market research into investment-box, sourcing, or screening changes.

## When to Use
When the firm wants a market thesis to change acquisition behavior rather than sit as a research memo.

## Process

### Phase 1: Diagnosis

Read `_shared-config/firm-profile.md`, `_shared-config/voice-and-tone.md` at full depth, and `_shared-config/learnings.md` sections `## General` and `## market-thesis-to-investment-box`.

Ask one question at a time:

1. Which market, sector, strategy, or theme is being evaluated?
2. Which data providers and research sources are approved?
3. What current investment-box criteria could change?
4. Who approves a sourcing or screening change?
5. Which downstream workspaces should consume the update?

### Phase 2: Assembly

Copy `architectures/market-thesis-to-investment-box/` to `workspaces/<name>/`. Populate `_config/research-standards.md`, `_config/current-investment-box.md`, and `_config/before-you-trust-this.md`.

Load and name constraints: 06, 09, 01, 02, 10.

### Phase 3: Orientation

Explain that the thesis is not done until it names the investment-box update and downstream owner.
