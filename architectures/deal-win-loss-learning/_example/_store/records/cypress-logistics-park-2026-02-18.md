# Stored Record: Cypress Logistics Park

Deal: Cypress Logistics Park
Tags: Industrial | $75M–$250M | Off-market | Developer | Eastgate Capital Markets | Inland Empire (Eastvale)
Outcome: WON ($112.0M)   Resolved: 2026-02-18
Our bid vs. clearing: We bid $112.0M; a competing group bid ~$114.5M. We won ~$2.5M *below* the
top nominal bid.
Decisive dimension: Certainty / speed of close
Validated by: D. Reyes, 2026-02-20

## Canonical Answers (summary)
Off-market look brought by Eastgate ahead of a marketed process. Value-add logistics thesis,
mark-to-market on below-market leases. We bid $112.0M with a 30-day close, no financing
contingency, and a hard deposit day one. A later group came in ~$2.5M higher but with a 60-day
close and a financing out. Seller (a merchant developer needing a clean Q1 close) took our
certainty over the higher number. We reached the principal directly through Eastgate.

## Validated Why
Won on certainty/speed of close, not price (HIGH). The seller's clean-close need was the lever;
our no-contingency, 30-day, hard-deposit package was decisively more certain than a higher bid
with a financing out. The off-market path through Eastgate gave us the read on what the seller
actually valued.

## Stated vs. Assessed
Aligned: Eastgate stated explicitly that the seller chose certainty over the marginal $2.5M, and
the bid structures corroborate it.

## Transferable Lessons
- A clean, no-contingency, hard-deposit package wins clean-close sellers even below top price —
  protect the balance sheet that lets us bid this way.
- Off-market access through a trusted intermediary lets us read the seller's real priority before
  pricing — Eastgate earned more of our early looks.
