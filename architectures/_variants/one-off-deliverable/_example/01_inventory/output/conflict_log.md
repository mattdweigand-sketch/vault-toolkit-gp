# Conflict Log: Maple Commons Hold/Sell Case

Where two sources disagree on a number, date, or decision. Both sides quoted. The pass surfaces
the disagreement and flags whether a human must resolve it. It does not pick a winner.

## CONFLICT-01 — Exit cap rate (and the value that flows from it)

**The disagreement:**
- **S02** (model v3, superseded): exit cap **5.25%**, implying a gross sale value of ~**$60M**.
- **S03** (model FINAL, current): exit cap **5.50%**, implying a gross sale value of ~**$57M**.
- **S04** (CBRE BOV, background): implied cap ~**5.40%**, value range **$58–62M**.

A 25 bps swing in exit cap moves the value by roughly $3M on this asset and changes the hold/sell
math materially — it is most of the gap between "sell now looks good" and "the hold case is
close."

**Which looks more authoritative:** S03 is the current model and supersedes S02 (see duplicate
log), so on version grounds the 5.50% is the firm's latest view. But that does not make 5.50%
*correct* — the change from 5.25% to 5.50% between versions is undocumented, and the email thread
(S06) hints the assumption was revised after a conversation with the broker. The BOV's ~5.40%
sits between the two.

**Resolution needs a human.** Why did the exit cap move 25 bps between v3 and FINAL, and which
reflects the committee's actual view of where this submarket trades? This is an IC-level
assumption, not something the inventory or the draft can settle. **Flagged for the deal lead to
confirm with the underwriter before the memo is finalized.**

**Until resolved:** the draft uses S03's 5.50% as the current model figure, labels it as the
current assumption (not a settled fact), shows the value's sensitivity to the cap, and surfaces
this conflict in its Open Items. It does **not** average the two caps into a single number.

## CONFLICT-02 — Value: appraisal vs. current views

**The disagreement:**
- **S07** (2024 appraisal, superseded): **$54M**.
- **S03 / S04** (current model and BOV): **$57–62M**.

**Which looks more authoritative:** the current views. S07 is two years old and pre-dates the
current business plan and the post-renovation rent gains in S05.

**Resolution:** none needed. This is not a live conflict — it is a stale figure correctly
outranked by the authority ladder. S07's $54M is **not** cited as current. Noted here only so a
reader who finds the appraisal in the pile understands why the memo does not use its number.
