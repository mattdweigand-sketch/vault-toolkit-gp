# Stored Record: Maple Grove Apartments

Deal: Maple Grove Apartments
Tags: Multifamily | $25M–$75M | Value-add | Tucson | 2–4 yr hold | Vintage 2020
Outcome: OUTPERFORMED (marginally)   Realized: 2024-03-15
Model of record: IC model v2 (final), approved 2020-02-11
Headline return: Underwritten 15.0% IRR / 1.7x → Realized 16.5% IRR / 1.8x (+1.5 pts / +0.1x)
Decisive driver: Exit cap rate (the beat); lease-up / opex (the offsetting drag)
Cause class: Exogenous (cap) offset by forecasting error + execution (lease-up, opex)
Skill / luck: **Luck-dominant**
Validated by: D. Reyes + M. Okafor, 2024-03-21

## Material Assumptions: Underwritten vs. Actual
- Exit cap rate: 5.50% → 5.05% (−45 bps, compressed)
- Lease-up / stabilization: 12 mo → 22 mo (+10 mo, badly slow — renovation delays)
- Opex growth: 3.0%/yr → 5.4%/yr (+2.4 pts, ran hot — insurance and payroll)
- Rent growth: 3.0%/yr → 3.1%/yr (in line)
- Capex: $16.0k → $17.8k/unit (+$1.8k, over on the delayed reno)

## Validated Why
The marginal beat is misleading. A −45 bps exit-cap compression *should* have driven a much larger
outperformance; it didn't, because our own execution ate most of it — stabilization ran 10 months
long on renovation delays and opex ran well over plan. [exogenous beat offset by forecasting +
execution misses; HIGH] Without the cap tailwind this deal underperforms its underwriting clearly.

## Skill vs. Luck
Luck-dominant [HIGH] — and the most sobering record in the store: even *with* a market tailwind, our
own misses nearly erased it. The lease-up miss (+10 mo) is the same direction as Cedar Crossing,
larger.

## Calibration Adjustment
- Lease-up (MF value-add, full reno): our 12-month assumption is materially optimistic — second
  record showing it (Cedar +6, Maple +10). Move toward ~18–22 months for full-reno plans.
- Opex growth (MF): 3.0% underwriting was light for the insurance/payroll environment; revisit.
- Exit cap (MF value-add): consistent with the no-compression calibration from Cedar.
