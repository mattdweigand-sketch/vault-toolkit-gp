## Voice
[How the firm speaks to LPs in a response. Keep it short and testable.

## What IR Answers Directly
[The inquiry types IR resolves without referral. Examples:
- Capital-account balance and contribution/distribution history (from the platform).
- Standard performance figures already reported.
- Document re-sends of items the LP is entitled to.
- Contact and entity detail updates.]

## What Gets Referred (and to whom)
[The inquiry types IR does NOT answer alone. Examples:
- Redemption, withdrawal, or transfer intent → IR principal + counsel.
- Complaints or disputes over a figure → IR principal + compliance.
- Side-letter interpretation or MFN questions → counsel.
- Forward-looking or return-expectation questions → IR principal; never
  answered with a projection.
- Commitment-increase or co-investment interest → IR principal / capital
  raising.]

## Never-Do List
[Testable rules every response must pass. Examples:
- Never state a figure not retrieved from the platform of record.
- Never release account information to an unconfirmed requester.
- Never make a forward-looking or performance projection.
- Never resolve a sensitive inquiry without the named owner.]
