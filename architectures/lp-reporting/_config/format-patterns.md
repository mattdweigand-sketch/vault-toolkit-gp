## Quarterly Letter
[Describe the structure: opening summary, performance, portfolio activity,
market view, what's next. Typical length. What makes your letter different
from a generic one? Example: "Lead with the period result and net IRR. One
paragraph per asset or segment with the key operating metric. Address the
single biggest LP concern head-on. Close with the next 90 days, not a
summary."]

## Capital Call Notice
[Describe the structure: purpose, amount, allocation reference, due date,
wire reference. Tight and unambiguous. Example: "State the call amount and
purpose in the first line. Reference the per-LP allocation schedule. Give the
due date and funding instructions reference. No narrative beyond the purpose."]

## Distribution Notice
[Describe the structure: source, amount, allocation reference, tax character,
payment date. Example: "State the distribution amount and source event. Note
the tax character at a high level with the standard disclaimer. Reference the
per-LP schedule and payment date."]

## Capital Account Statement Cover
[Describe any cover note that accompanies statements. Example: "Brief.
Reference the period, point to the statement detail, note who to contact with
questions. No performance commentary, the statement carries the figures."]
