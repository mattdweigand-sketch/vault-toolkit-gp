## How to read this table

- **Authority drives citation.** The draft cites S01 for current basis/debt/NAV, S03 for forward
  math, S05 for the operating picture. It does not cite S02 or S07 as current — they are
  superseded and kept only for history.
- **Relevance drives effort.** The high-relevance sources (S01, S03, S05) get full summaries.
  Low-relevance ones (S02, S06, S07) get a line here and nothing more.
- **The two unresolved items** — the exit-cap conflict (S02 vs. S03) and the missing rent roll
  (S08) — are logged separately and must surface in the draft, not be smoothed over.

## What this inventory does NOT do

It does not pick the exit cap. It does not reconcile the BOV value against the model value. It
does not decide hold vs. sell. Those are judgments for the model, the platform, and the IC. The
inventory classifies and flags; it never reconciles or computes (Constraint 10, boundary with
Constraint 09).
