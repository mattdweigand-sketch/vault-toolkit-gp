# S03 — Maple Commons Financial Model, FINAL (~2026-05-15)

- **Type:** Financial model (working tool). **Authority:** Supporting (current version of the
  model family; supersedes S02).
- **Trustworthy for:** the forward projections and the hold/sell return math — *as the firm's
  current modeled view*, not as a settled fact. It is a working model, not the book of record.

## What it contains
- **Sell scenario (Q3 2026):** exit cap 5.50%, gross value ~$57M, net proceeds to the fund after
  the $31.0M debt payoff (S01) and transaction costs.
- **Hold scenario (to 2028):** continued lease-up and rent growth per the business plan (S05), a
  $1.1M capital reserve over the hold, exit at the same 5.50% cap in 2028.
- **In-place income** driven by a February 2026 rent roll cited in the assumptions tab.

## Flags carried into the draft
- **Exit cap is contested (CONFLICT-01).** S02 used 5.25%; this version uses 5.50%; the change is
  undocumented. The 5.50% is the current assumption, not a settled fact. The draft labels it as
  such and shows value sensitivity to the cap.
- **In-place income rests on an absent source (MISSING-01).** The February rent roll (S08) is
  referenced but not in the pile.
- **Capital reserve basis undocumented (MISSING-03).**
