## The Questions
[A fixed list the analysis answers for every engagement, commit or pass.
Write 8-15 specific questions. Examples to adapt:

1. How did this LP first enter the pipeline, and through whom?
2. What was the LP's stated mandate / allocation thesis for this fund?
3. Which of our materials or moments most moved the engagement forward?
4. What objections or concerns did the LP raise, and in what order?
5. What was the real decision driver, as distinct from the stated reason?
6. Who on the LP side held the decision, and did we reach them?
7. Where did the engagement stall or lose momentum, if it did?
8. What did timing / fund terms / track record contribute to the outcome?
9. If passed: what, if anything, would have changed the outcome?
10. If committed: what sealed it, and what nearly lost it?
11. What would we do differently with a similar LP next time?]

## Answering Rules
[How to answer for comparability:
- Answer every question, in order. Use "unknown" where the record does not
  support an answer — never fabricate to fill the slot.
- Keep answers tight and factual; the why and the lessons have their own
  sections in the analysis output.
- Distinguish stated reasons from assessed reasons explicitly.]
