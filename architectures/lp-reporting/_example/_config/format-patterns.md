# Format Patterns — Meridian Real Estate Partners

Structure for each report type. L3 reference. The draft stage reads the section matching what
it is producing.

## Quarterly Letter

1. **Opening summary** — one paragraph. Net IRR and net multiple for the fund, the quarter's
   single most important development, and the headline for the period.
2. **Performance** — fund-level returns this quarter and since inception. A short table.
3. **Portfolio activity** — one paragraph per asset, in order of materiality. What changed,
   what it means, what the plan is.
4. **Market view** — two to three sentences. Only what is relevant to the portfolio.
5. **What's next** — the next 90 days. Concrete items, not aspirations.
6. **Disclosures** — the required footer block (see constraints.md).

Length: two to three pages. Meridian's LPs read the whole thing; do not pad it.

## Capital Call Notice

Purpose and amount in the first line. Per-LP allocation reference. Due date. Wire instructions
reference. No narrative.

## Distribution Notice

Source and amount first. Per-LP allocation reference. Tax character note. Payment date.
