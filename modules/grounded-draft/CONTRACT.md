# Grounded Draft Contract

## Purpose
Produce a draft whose factual claims can be traced to reviewed sources.

## Inputs
- Reviewed source inventory or fact pack.
- Deliverable spec.
- Voice and tone reference.
- Drafting standards.

## Process
1. Draft only from approved facts, source IDs, and labeled inferences.
2. Cite factual claims with source ID, fact ID, or platform reference.
3. Label inferences and confidence.
4. Put unsupported claims in open items, not prose.
5. Preserve conflicts that matter to the artifact.

## Outputs
- Draft artifact.
- `source_usage_map.md`
- `open_items.md`

## Done Looks Like
Every factual claim cites a source. Inferences are labeled. Unsupported claims, conflicts, and missing facts remain visible.

## Common Failure Modes
- Fluent unsupported prose.
- Source citations that point to weak or superseded files.
- Hiding uncertainty because it disrupts the narrative.

## Layer Annotation
L2 module contract. Drafts and usage maps are L4 outputs.
