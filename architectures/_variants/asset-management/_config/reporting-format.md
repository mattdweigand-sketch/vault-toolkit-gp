## Variant: JV / Co-GP Partner Report
[Same underlying analysis, reframed for a co-sponsor or operating partner.
Describe the differences from the base:
- Audience: a sophisticated co-investor, not an LP and not the internal IC.
- Adds: promote/waterfall position, decision-rights context, more granular
  asset-level operating detail.
- Tone: peer-to-peer, direct, assumes operating fluency.
- Same figures and same analysis as the base review — only framing and tone
  change. Never a different number for a different audience.]

## Variant: Watchlist / Special-Servicing Memo
[An intensified review of a single distressed or underperforming asset.
Describe the differences:
- Scope: one asset, deep.
- Adds: the cure/extend/recapitalize options on the table, the downside case,
  the decision the IC faces and by when.
- Tone: direct on bad news, action-oriented, no softening.
- Still narrates and flags; the return/valuation math behind any option comes
  from the model (Constraint 09).
Note: if workout becomes the firm's primary business, this variant graduates
to its own gated decision workspace.]
