# Missing Context: Maple Commons Hold/Sell Case

Claims with no supporting source, references to documents not present, and numbers with no stated
basis. The draft flags these rather than filling them in.

## MISSING-01 — The rent roll the model is built on (S08)
S03's assumptions tab references a "rent roll as of 2026-02," but no rent-roll file is in the
pile. The model's in-place income — the foundation of every forward number — therefore cannot be
verified against a source. The occupancy in S05 (the AM report) is consistent with a healthy
in-place picture, but it is not the rent roll and does not tie to the model line by line.
**Impact:** the hold/sell math rests on an in-place income figure the inventory cannot confirm.
**Action:** request the February rent roll from asset management before the memo is finalized. The
draft flags the in-place income as resting on an unverified source.

## MISSING-02 — A refinance quote referenced but not present
The email thread (S06) mentions "the refi quote we got" as a reason the hold case might be
stronger than it looks — implying a financing path that could improve hold-period returns. No
refinance term sheet or quote is in the pile. **Impact:** the hold case may understate returns if
a real refinance is available, but there is no source to support including it. **Action:** the
draft notes the hold case excludes any refinance because none is sourced, and flags the quote as
worth retrieving if the IC wants the hold case fully developed.

## MISSING-03 — Capital reserve assumption has no stated basis
S03 carries a $1.1M capital reserve over the hold period, but neither the model notes nor any
other source explains how it was derived (a PCA? a per-unit rule of thumb? the original
underwriting?). **Impact:** modest, but it is a number with no provenance feeding the hold case.
**Action:** the draft cites it as the model's assumption and flags that its basis is not
documented.

## Not missing, just worth stating
The current basis, debt balance, and NAV (S01) are fully sourced to fund admin and need nothing
further. The operating picture (S05) is current and complete. The gaps above are specific and
bounded — they do not undermine the deliverable, but the IC should know the in-place income
(MISSING-01) is the one load-bearing figure resting on an absent source.
