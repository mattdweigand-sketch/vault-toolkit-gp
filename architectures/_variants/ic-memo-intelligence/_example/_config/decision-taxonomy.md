# Decision Taxonomy — Ridgeline Capital Partners

The controlled vocabulary for tagging each record so the store aggregates into decision precedent.
Stable since v1.0 (2022-06).

## Asset Type
multifamily · industrial *(Ridgeline invests only in these two)*

## Deal-Size Band
< $25M · $25M–$75M · $75M–$250M · > $250M

## Market / Submarket
Phoenix (Tempe, Mesa) · Tucson · Las Vegas · Inland Empire · Reno *(our active markets)*

## Strategy
core-plus · value-add · opportunistic *(value-add is the bulk of the book)*

## Decision Lane
Approved · Approved-with-conditions · Declined · Tabled

## Condition Category
Debt-service / DSCR stress test · Leverage cap / max LTV · Reserve or contingency requirement ·
Sponsor / guarantor terms · Third-party report · Lease-up / pre-leasing milestone ·
Pricing / structure change before close · Reporting / monitoring requirement

## Concern Category
Market / submarket softness · Leverage / financing risk · Execution / sponsor capability ·
Basis / pricing · Lease-up / absorption risk · Tenant / credit concentration · Exit / liquidity risk ·
Capital-markets / rate exposure

## Decisive Factor
The single thing that drove the decision. Tag the primary one; note a secondary in the record.

## Dissent
none · stated reservation · formal no-vote

## Precedent Relationship
sets-precedent · consistent-with-precedent · departs-from-precedent
