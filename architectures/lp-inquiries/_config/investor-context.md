# Investor Context

## Investor Entities and Contacts
[Per investor: legal entity name(s), the authorized contacts and their roles,
preferred channel, and which fund(s) they are in. Used at intake to identify
the requester and confirm authority.]

## Standing Sensitivities
[Anything that should shape how an inquiry from this LP is handled. Examples:
an LP in a dispute, an LP who escalates quickly, an LP with a side letter that
affects what they can be told, a relationship a partner manages personally.]

## Inquiry History
[Recurring or notable prior inquiries per investor, so a follow-up is handled
with context rather than from scratch. Grows over time.]

## Routing Map
[Who owns what when an inquiry must be referred: the IR principal, compliance
contact, counsel, the capital-raising lead. Used by intake and resolve to route
escalations to a named person, not a vague "someone."]
