# Canonical IC Questions — Ridgeline Capital Partners

The fixed set every Ridgeline IC record answers, in this order, every time. This is what makes the
records comparable. Stable since v1.0 (2022-06); any change is versioned and dated.

## The Questions
1. What was the recommendation, what did the committee decide (approved / approved-with-conditions /
   declined / tabled), and did the decision diverge from the recommendation?
2. What conditions did the committee impose, to whom, and by when?
3. What concerns or risks did the committee raise, in its own words where on the record?
4. What was the decisive factor — the one thing that, if different, most likely flips the decision?
   Causal or incidental?
5. Stated vs. inferred rationale: what reasons do the minutes attribute to the committee, and what
   is our assessment of what actually drove the decision? (Keep the two distinct.)
6. Was there dissent, and on what grounds — a formal no-vote or a stated reservation?
7. Are the conditions/concerns the committee's standing guardrails (recurring per the store) or
   bespoke to this deal?
8. Is this decision consistent with precedent, or a departure?
9. What does this reveal about the committee's risk appetite in this segment?
10. What should a future memo in this segment pre-empt, address, or cite?

## Answering Rules
- Answer every question, in order. Use "not on the record" where the minutes do not support an
  answer — never fabricate to fill the slot.
- Keep the committee's stated reasoning distinct from our inferred reading; never collapse them.
- Mark standing vs. bespoke on every condition and concern, so a one-deal quirk does not get read
  as the committee's policy.
