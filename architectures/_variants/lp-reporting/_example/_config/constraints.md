# Constraints — Meridian Real Estate Partners

The never-do list and the required-always list for LP communications. L3 reference. Loaded in
stages 02 and 03. These were confirmed with compliance on 2026-01-15. Re-confirm annually.

## Required on every communication

- **Disclosure footer** (verbatim): "This communication is intended solely for the recipient
  and contains confidential information. Past performance is not indicative of future
  results. This is not an offer to sell or a solicitation to buy any security."
- **Performance basis line:** state whether figures are gross or net of fees and carry. Meridian
  reports **net** to LPs in quarterly letters.
- **As-of date** on all performance figures.

## Forward-looking language

- Projections are qualified and grounded. "We expect to stabilize Cedar Crossing by Q3" is
  acceptable. "Cedar Crossing will deliver a 16% IRR" is not.
- No guarantees of return, distribution timing, or exit outcome.
- Frame business-plan items as plans, not promises.

## Never

- No figure that does not trace to `01_data/output/data-pack.md`.
- No comparison to a benchmark unless the benchmark is named and the basis matches.
- No LP-specific figure in a fund-wide letter; per-LP figures go through the per-LP merge in
  stage 03.
- No em dashes in investor-facing copy.
