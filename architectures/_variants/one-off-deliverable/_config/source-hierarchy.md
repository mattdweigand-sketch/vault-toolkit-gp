## Authority ladder
- **Authoritative** — the book of record. [List the sources that qualify, once known.]
- **Supporting** — adds context but is not the source of truth (an analyst summary, a working model).
- **Background** — relevant but not load-bearing (a market study, prior correspondence).
- **Superseded** — kept for history, never cited as current (the draft a final replaced).
