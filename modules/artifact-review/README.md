# Artifact Review

Runs a skeptical review after a draft, deck, workbook, memo, handoff brief, or decision packet exists.

Use this before an artifact is shared, relied on, or handed to another workspace. Do not use it to rewrite the artifact. The review enumerates issues and names the owner or human decision required.

Implements constraints 02, 08, 09, and 10.

Used by source-backed deliverables, LP narrative, market thesis, hold/sell/refi, portfolio intervention, and any workflow that produces a polished artifact or downstream handoff.
