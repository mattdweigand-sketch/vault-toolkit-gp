# IC Decision Analysis: Cedar Crossing Apartments
Record reference: 01_record/output/record-cedar-crossing-2019-06-04.md   Decision: Approved-with-conditions

## Canonical Questions
1. **Recommendation vs. decision:** Memo recommended approval at 68% LTV; committee approved but cut
   leverage to 65% and attached a DSCR stress test and a $1.5M reserve. Decision diverged from the
   recommendation on structure, not on the go/no-go.
2. **Conditions:** Leverage cap (65% LTV), DSCR stress test (+200 bps, ≥1.20x), $1.5M reserve.
3. **Concerns (committee's words):** Lease-up — "twelve months… is the optimistic end; what happens
   if it's eighteen?" Leverage — "not comfortable at 68% on a value-add plan in this rate environment."
4. **Decisive factor:** The basis — going-in price below replacement cost — carried the approval.
   Causal: had the basis been ordinary, the lease-up and leverage doubts would likely have tipped it
   to a decline or table.
5. **Stated vs. inferred rationale:**
   - *Stated (minutes):* approved on the basis and the sponsor's track record.
   - *Inferred (our read):* the basis did the work; the committee was **not** comfortable with the
     12-month lease-up assumption and managed that discomfort with conditions (reserve + DSCR
     stress) rather than by declining. The "sponsor track record" line is not supported as decisive
     — the minutes record a discussion but not a conclusion (see record Gaps). Confidence: medium-high
     on the basis read; low on sponsor-track-record as a driver.
6. **Dissent:** One stated reservation (Okafor, lease-up). No formal no-vote.
7. **Standing vs. bespoke:** The DSCR stress test and the leverage cap look like standing guardrails,
   not deal-specific — worth watching across the next MF approvals. The $1.5M reserve size is bespoke.
8. **Precedent:** First record in the store — sets-precedent.
9. **Risk appetite revealed:** The committee will approve a strong-basis value-add MF deal but not at
   68% leverage; ~65% reads as its comfort line here, and it prices lease-up risk through conditions.
10. **For future memos:** Bring value-add MF at ≤65% LTV, include a DSCR stress case in the memo, and
    address the lease-up timeline directly with a downside (18-month) sensitivity.

## Stated vs. Inferred Rationale
Stated: "basis and sponsor track record." Inferred: basis-driven approval *despite* unresolved
lease-up doubt, offloaded into conditions. Do not record this as a confident, comfortable approval —
the conditions are the tell that the committee was managing risk it could not underwrite away.

Basis (below replacement cost). Causal. The conditions, not the approval, carry the committee's
actual concerns.
