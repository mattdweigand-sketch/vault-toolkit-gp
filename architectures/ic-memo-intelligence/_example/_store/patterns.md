## REVEALED RISK APPETITE (the boundaries it will not cross)

### R1 — Hard leverage ceiling at ~65% LTV; the committee declines rather than stretch, even on a strong basis
**Segment:** value-add MF (all records so far).
**Evidence:** 3 records. **STATED.**
- Cedar Crossing: recommended 68%, approved at 65%.
- Maple Grove: recommended 67%, approved at 65%.
- Stonebridge: recommended 72%, **declined** — leverage was the binding constraint on a basis the
  committee called compelling.
**Confidence:** High. The boundary holds across approve and decline, and across a 4-year span and a
rising-rate turn. This is the single most reliable thing the store knows about the committee.
**For the memo / screening:** do not advance value-add MF above 65% LTV expecting a stretch.
Restructure to the ceiling before IC. (Flows to deal-pipeline IC-memo template and deal-screening.)

## RECURRING CONCERNS (what it raises again and again)

### N1 — Lease-up / absorption risk on full-renovation MF
**Segment:** multifamily value-add, full-reno scope.
**Evidence:** 3 records. **STATED.** Raised on Cedar, Maple, and Stonebridge — every full-reno MF deal.
**Last updated:** 2023-08-17.

## DECISION PRECEDENTS (notable approvals and declines to measure new deals against)

- **The value-add MF approval template (Cedar Crossing, Maple Grove):** strong basis + ≤65% LTV +
  DSCR stress + lease-up managed by reserve or milestone = the route through committee.
- **Stonebridge (declined, 72% LTV, Las Vegas, 2023-08-15):** the standing precedent that leverage
  is a hard line, not a negotiating point. Cite it when a sponsor pushes for higher leverage.
