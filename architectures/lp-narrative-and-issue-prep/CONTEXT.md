# Workflow: LP Narrative And Issue Prep

## Overview
Four-stage investor-communication workflow: Facts -> Narrative -> Questions -> Posture.

## Modules Used
- `modules/verified-fact-pack/CONTRACT.md`: stage 01 fact pack and forbidden claims.
- `modules/grounded-draft/CONTRACT.md`: stage 02 source-backed narrative.
- `modules/response-posture/CONTRACT.md`: stage 04 answer, caveat, hold, escalate, or route posture.
- `modules/handoff-brief/CONTRACT.md`: recurring objection or issue handoff into firm memory.

## Stage Map

| Stage | Purpose | Inputs | Output Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01_facts | Gather platform-verified fact pack and forbidden claims | Investor platform, reporting pack, issue source | 01_facts/output/ |
| 02_narrative | Draft grounded explanation around verified facts | Fact pack, voice, constraints | 02_narrative/output/ |
| 03_questions | Prepare likely LP questions | Narrative, investor context, prior objections | 03_questions/output/ |
| 04_posture | Set approved response posture, escalation, and memory handoff if useful | Questions, standards, owners | 04_posture/output/ |

## AI vs. Platform

| Step | Layer | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| LP records, figures, DDQ, portal, entitlement, audit | Platform | Juniper Square or investor platform |
| Template routing and approval thresholds | Rule-based | IR / compliance standards |
| Narrative, question prep, sensitive framing | AI | IR team |
| Approval and sending | Human | IR lead / compliance |
